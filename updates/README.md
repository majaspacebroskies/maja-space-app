# Maja Space: update files

This folder holds the update files of the Maja Space apps (Android, Windows, Mac), made by Mohamed Safi.
Installed apps check `update.json` here by themselves and update; there is nothing to do here for students.

**Get the app:** https://majaspace.info/

- Android phones and tablets (Android 7 or newer): [MajaSpace-latest.apk](https://github.com/majaspacebroskies/maja-space-app/raw/main/updates/MajaSpace-latest.apk) (version 10.6, content 10.6.0, published 2026-11-09).
- Windows and Mac: [the latest release](https://github.com/majaspacebroskies/maja-space-app/releases/latest).

What's new: New look: three themes (Aurora, now the default, Midnight, and Ink & Gold with gold gradients), smoother type, a new sign-in page, a full-screen menu, and a timer you drag to set and slide to start, with End early by holding. Pick the timer's end sound on the timer card, or your own song, which plays to its end (Stop on screen). The app lock always locks Instagram, YouTube, X and Snapchat along with the apps you choose, and says so when it turns on. Guides for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English and Tamil, next to the Ram Maths Guide. New: full mock papers from real public papers (3 hours, then you mark them), derivations step by step, a scholarship and 7.5% quota checker, Share my week for parents, how many members are studying now, and one-mark practice for every chapter (906 questions with the answer and why). Sign in with Google or your email: your study data stays in your account, on every device. Room for 60 members; members 1 to 30 have new passwords (sign in once with the new one, your progress stays). Sync uploads less and no longer puts a device's older settings back. Exam dates, JEE and NEET dates, the paper list and weightage now update by themselves from the website. Vibration on taps and when you finish things. On Android, Phone use (Tools): your screen time, the apps you use most, every app you opened, the data each app used and a weekly report, kept on your phone only and off until you switch it on. Engineering colleges: every TNEA college, branch and community, with closing marks 2022 to 2026. Mock tests and the Mistake notebook are gone (full mock papers keep their scores). Exam dates checked against DGE's own timetable. Maja Space is made by Mohamed Safi.

Every file here is signed with Maja Space's key. The apps ignore anything that isn't, so a changed or fake
file can't be installed through them.
